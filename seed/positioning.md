# Positioning and voice

## The angle

A product leader who comes to AI product management from 14 years of enterprise UX,
not from a PM title. The strength is making AI features usable, trusted and coherent
inside complex, regulated products. Lead with that; never apologise for the path.

## Honest gaps (do not paper over these)

- No role with the formal title "Product Manager". Product ownership has been exercised through UX leadership, consulting and a co-founded startup.
- No direct ownership of ML model training or evaluation pipelines.
- Based in India: needs roles open to worldwide, EMEA or US-overlap remote hiring.

## Voice

- Plain, direct, warm. Short sentences. British/Indian English spelling.
- Specific over impressive: name the project and the result.
- No buzzwords: avoid "passionate", "synergy", "leverage", "cutting-edge", "thrilled", "excited to apply".
- No flattery of the company beyond one concrete, researched observation.
- First person, active voice. No exclamation marks.
