# Draft for a shortlisted job

Use only what `profile.md` and `positioning.md` actually state, plus the company
brief and hook from `research-company`. Never invent experience, metrics,
employers or titles.

## `email_context` (short — a few lines, not a full email)

The specific details someone would need to write or approve an outreach email
themselves: a subject-line idea, the one concrete hook that makes this outreach
not generic, and who to address it to if known (role or name, from
`research-company`). Not a drafted email body — just the ammunition for one.

## `cover_note` (one short paragraph, a few sentences)

Why this profile fits this specific role, grounded in one or two concrete
points from the resume. No greeting, no sign-off, no filler.

Both fields: plain text, no placeholders like `[Name]` or `{{company}}` — if a
detail isn't known, leave it out rather than guessing.
